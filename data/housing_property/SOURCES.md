# SOURCES - housing_property (accessed 2026-11-14; all publisher = Swiss Federal Statistical Office BFS/FSO)
Script: /home/user/Diaspora/scripts/housing_property_parse.py (parses the raw files in raw/). Raw files kept in raw/.

1. tenure_by_household_nationality_grossregion.csv - Table "Privathaushalte nach Nationalität der Haushaltsmitglieder und Bewohnertyp, nach Grossregionen" (je-d-09.03.02.01.08 / T 09.03.02.01.08), 2012-2022. URL: https://dam-api.bfs.admin.ch/hub/api/dam/assets/30885334/master (listed on https://statistik.admin.ch/de/news/2024-0421). Source: Strukturerhebung (SE) + Gebäude- und Wohnungsstatistik (GWS). Universe: private households of the permanent resident population; household nationality = Swiss household (all members Swiss) / mixed (>=1 Swiss and >=1 foreigner) / foreign household (all members foreign). NO split by country: Romania NOT separate. Share % with 95% CI half-width. "Andere Situation" = free housing from relative/employer, service flat, tenant farmer.
2. avg_net_rent_by_household_nationality_grossregion.csv - "Durchschnittlicher Mietpreis in Franken nach Nationalität der Haushaltsmitglieder und Zimmerzahl, nach Grossregionen" (je-d-09.03.03.10), 2012-2022. URL: https://dam-api.bfs.admin.ch/hub/api/dam/assets/30885421/master. Monthly net rent (no utilities/heating) of tenant and cooperative flats; same 3 nationality groups.
3. avg_net_rent_by_canton_rooms.csv - "Durchschnittlicher Mietpreis in Franken nach Zimmerzahl und Kanton" (je-d-09.03.03.01), years 2000, 2003, 2010-2024. URL: https://dam-api.bfs.admin.ch/hub/api/dam/assets/36398431/master. Caveat: method changes 2015 (questionnaire) and 2018 (survey method/weighting) - series only partly comparable; Strukturerhebung covers persons 15+ in private households. Only years/rows parsed where numeric.
4. home_ownership_rate_by_canton.csv - "Wohneigentumsquote, nach Kanton - 2019-2023" (ts-x-09.03.02.01.03b). URL: https://dam-api.bfs.admin.ch/hub/api/dam/assets/33947654/master (CSV; NUTS code mapped to canton abbreviation by me). Share of occupied dwellings that are owner-occupied. Note: a newer version (to 2024) exists ("Bewohnte Wohnungen nach Bewohnertyp, nach Kanton 2019-2024", ts-x-09.03.02.01.03a, https://www.bfs.admin.ch/asset/de/ts-x-09.03.02.01.03a) - not downloaded (budget).
5. living_space_per_person_by_migration_status_canton.csv - Integration indicator "Durchschnittliche Wohnfläche pro Person" tables su-d-01.05.07.06.02.01 (1-person households) and su-d-01.05.07.06.02.02 (2+ persons), 2013-2024, by canton and rooms. URLs: https://dam-api.bfs.admin.ch/hub/api/dam/assets/36680442/master and .../36680464/master; indicator page https://statistik.admin.ch/de/themen/bevoelkerung/integration/integrationsindikatoren/durchschnittliche-wohnflaeche-pro-person. Sources: GWS, STATPOP. Household migration status (household level: with / without migration background / mixed), not nationality. Rooms: total,1..6plus.
