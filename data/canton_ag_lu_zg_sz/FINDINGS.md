# Findings: AG, LU, ZG, SZ
- AG 2025-12-31: 3 941 Romanian nationals (2 300 men, 1 641 women) in cantonal population statistics (Tab. 22, https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/statistik/publikationen/statistikthemen/01-bevoelkerung/2025/edossier-bev-lkerung-2025-jahrespublikation.xlsx).
- BFS STATPOP permanent residents AG 2025: 3 781; cantonal figure +160 (+4.23%). Likely reasons (not verified in a source): cantonal definition counts Ausweis S/N/F after 12 months and register-based differences; BFS reference is permit-total permanent pop.
- BFS reference AG: 312 (2010), 606 (2015), 1 234 (2019), 1 548 (2020), 3 781 (2025). LU: 163, 379, 608, 714, 1 741. ZG: 75, 181, 310, 339, 538. SZ: 82, 161, 334, 394, 627.
- AG arrivals from Romania (all nationalities): 141 (2018), 276 (2019), 299 (2020), 349 (2021), 573 (2022), 699 (2023), 677 (2024), 686 (2025); full series 1973-2025 in ag_romanians_arrivals_1973_2025.csv.
- AG Romanian nationals: immigration 275 / 676 / 686 (2019 / 2024 / 2025); emigration 92 / 307 / 298; net 183 / 369 / 388 (2019/2024/2025). 2025: 462 men and 224 women arrived.
- In 2025 Romania was the 2nd largest net-migration source for AG (+387 all nationalities) after Germany (+2 341), ahead of Italy (+374) (Tab. 11c).
- 2025 AG total foreign population 209 264 of 743 643 (Bezirk table, 31.12.2025); no Romania split by Bezirk.
