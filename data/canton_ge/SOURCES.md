File base: https://statistique.ge.ch/statistique/tel/domaines/01/<domain>/<file>.xlsx . Raw copies in raw/. Scripts: /home/user/Diaspora/scripts/canton_ge_download.py, canton_ge_parse.py.
Definition (all stock tables): "population résidante" from the cantonal population statistics (OCSTAT, based on the cantonal/federal registers), situation at 31 December, by nationality. It INCLUDES international officials/diplomats (CI/EX permits), asylum domain (F/N), permit S and short-stay L. BFS "ständige Wohnbevölkerung" is narrower. Suppression: "( )" = withheld for data protection, "-" = zero.
