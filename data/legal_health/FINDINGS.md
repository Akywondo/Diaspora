## A. Lex Koller (BJ; FAQ Stand 08.09.2026)
- EU/EFTA nationals resident in CH (B, C, possibly L) are NOT "Personen im Ausland" -> no authorisation for any purchase (FAQ Q4 p.7, Q24 p.14). Romania is an EU member, so Romanian residents with L/B/C are covered.
- Residence must be lawful and actual (rechtmaessig und tatsaechlich); a valid L/B/C permit is the proof (FAQ Q10).
- Non-EU nationals are persons abroad unless C permit; they may buy a main residence without authorisation, but not second home/investment.
- G-permit EU/EFTA commuters: second home in workplace region without authorisation, personal name only (Q29 p.16, Q65 p.29).
- Leavers: an EU resident who moves abroad becomes a person abroad (Q14 example); see Q13, Q28, Q32 for sale duties.
- Revision: consultation opened 15.4.2026, until 15.7.2026; statements published 27.7.2026; aims at authorisation for third-country buyers and fewer holiday homes. No dispatch listed as of 15.11.2026 on the dossier page.
- Mandate to EJPD 29.1.2025 (companion measure to rejection of the 10-million initiative).
