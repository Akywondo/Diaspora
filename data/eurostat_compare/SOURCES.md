
## remittances_ro_from_ch.csv
- Publisher: Eurostat, dataset `bop_rem6` "Personal transfers and compensation of employees" (BPM6), last update 2025-12-15.
- URL: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/statistics/1.0/data/bop_rem6?format=JSON&geo=RO&partner=CH (accessed 2026-11-15).
- Universe: Romania's balance of payments as reported by Romania (BNR to Eurostat), partner Switzerland, credit side (inflows to Romania), million EUR, 2013-2024.
- Items: D752 personal transfers (= D752W workers' remittances here); D1 compensation of employees (residents of Romania working in CH for less than a year, incl. seasonal and cross-border); R1 personal remittances (D752 + net compensation of employees + capital transfers); R1WR workers' remittances and compensation of employees.
- Caveat: 2015-2018 D1 values are 0 (not compiled / confidential), so R1 jumps in 2021 partly by method.
