## Key facts
- FR: Romanian nationals (Dec, permanent + non-permanent foreign residents) 325 (2010), 559 (2015), 636 (2016), 692 (2017), 797 (2018), 846 (2019), 922 (2020), 1 030 (2021). Source: SStat Annuaire statistique 2020/2023, table T01-11 (URLs in SOURCES.md).
- FR long run: 1 (1950), 2 (1970), 161 (1990) (T01-11 edition 2023).
- FR (SStat web page, 2025): Romania +1 105 persons 2010-2025 among permanent residents, = BFS 1 343 - 238 exactly. https://www.fr.ch/deef/ssd/statistiques-par-themes/migration-et-integration
- FR 2025 total foreigners share 25.8 %; Portugal 27 700, France 15 400, Italy ~6 100 (same page). Romania's own 2025 level only via BFS: 1 343 permanent.
- SO (cantonal register, 31 Dec): Romanians 1 191 (2021, rank 15), 1 524 (2022, rank 13), 1 762 (2023, rank 12), 1 915 (2024), 2 052 (2025, 2.7 % of foreign pop. per SO table, 11th nationality). Sources: SO Wohnbevölkerung 2021-2023, Solothurn zählt! 2025/2026.
- SO 2025: 1 176 men, 876 women (43 % women); 2024: 1 041 m / 874 f (SO tables).
- SO 2025 growth of Romanians +9.1 % (one of the fastest-growing nationalities, with Afghanistan +11.4 %); SO 2024 +10.5 %.
- SO migration 2025: net migration balance vs Romania +145 persons (4th highest after Aargau +491, Bern +469, Germany +413; Solothurn zählt! 2026 p. 8).
- BFS (reference): permanent Romanians SO 137 (2010) -> 619 (2019) -> 1 857 (2025); BE 475 -> 1 798 -> 3 088; FR 238 -> 751 -> 1 343. Non-permanent SO 270 (2025).
- Why SO is large (BFS permit/sex data): from 2019 to 2025 SO gained +1 238 permanent Romanians = 6.0 % of the national increase (20 114 -> 40 627); growth is concentrated 2019-2023 (619, 807, 997, 1 287, 1 543). Composition shifted to recent arrivals: B permit 70 % (2019) -> 84 % (2025), C permit 18 % -> 12 %; men share 45 % (2019) -> 56 % (2025) vs 26 % in 2010 (male-dominated labour migration: new job-driven B permits, low family reunification). SO 2025 ranks 8th among cantons (ZH 8 429, AG 3 781, VD 3 765, SG 3 213, BE 3 088, GE 2 366, SO 1 857, TG 1 840), so "high for its size" is relative to population.
- Official data does NOT identify municipalities, sector or employer for Romanians in SO (see below), so the cause (e.g. logistics/industry/construction employment) cannot be proven from official cantonal data in my reach.

## Cross-checks done
- FR cantonal (Dec, perm + nonperm foreigners) vs BFS permanent+non-permanent: identical in all 8 years (diff 0) -> SStat republishes STATPOP.
- FR +1 105 (2010-2025) vs BFS permanent 1 343-238: identical.
- SO cantonal vs BFS permanent+non-permanent: -2.7 % to -4.9 % (2021-2025: -55, -78, -80, -54, -75); vs BFS permanent only: +10.5 % to +19.5 % (+194, +237, +219, +204, +195). Reason: SO counts all with main residence incl. L, F, N, S regardless of the 12-month rule, but its register evidently excludes part of BFS's non-permanent group (e.g. short stays, diplomats/officials etc.), so it sits between the two BFS aggregates. Also editions revise past values.
